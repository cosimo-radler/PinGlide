# Collection sources

The collection contains 26 distinct images: nine user-supplied works (including two animated GIFs), six original AI-created images, and eleven museum open-access works. User-supplied files remain unmodified at their original locations; website copies of still images are optimized for delivery. GIFs retain their animation, with still-frame fallbacks for reduced-motion preferences.

## Original Elsewhere posters

Three AI-generated posters commissioned for this website: **Direction**, **Space**, and **Capture**. Geometric compositions in ivory, cobalt, black and vermilion with the Elsewhere wordmark. These are original website brand studies, not attributed historical Bauhaus works. Delivery files: `elsewhere-direction.jpg`, `elsewhere-space.jpg`, and `elsewhere-capture.jpg`.
